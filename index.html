Terminologies & Definitions

DOM
Document Object Model — the structural tree representation of HTML elements in the browser.
Attribute
Additional values configured inside an opening tag to modify its behavior or meaning.
Accessibility (a11y)
The practice of making websites usable for people with diverse abilities and screen-reader devices.
